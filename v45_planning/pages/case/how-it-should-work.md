# Case record: Report wording, how it should work

Proposed on 2 November 2026 from the operator's brief and answers. D1–D6 come from the brief
and answers and are settled. The open lines wait for the sign-off list in
[v45-notes §6](../../notes/v45-notes.md#6-sign-off).

## Rules

- **D1.** The Valuation section no longer carries On the report. Every choice about what the
  report includes lives in the Report section's Report wording panel.
- **D2.** Report wording opens with one settings checklist.
  - It lists every offered wording block in print order, then the manual paragraphs, then
    Disclose guide source, FAO The Court and Include statement of truth.
  - Each item defaults as it does today:
    - the blocks are on;
    - Disclose guide source, Valuation commentary and Unrelated damage are off until
      recorded on;
    - FAO The Court and the statement are on until recorded off.
- **D3.** A wording block has no on/off control of its own. An item that is not ticked has no
  block in the panel. Ticking it shows the block.
- **D4.** PAV commentary and Unrelated damage are offered as blocks whatever their tick says.
  The commentary is written in its own block.
- **D5.** The Statement of truth is a read-only block in the panel, pinned last. Its separate
  folded sub-panel is removed.
- **D6.** Disclose guide source shows the Glass's guide sentence as a read-only box, in the
  place it prints within the statement. FAO The Court is a tick with no block.

## Open

These are the lettered items in the sign-off list.

- Open (**A**): the commentary and unrelated damage ticks write the existing assessment
  switches. Those two blocks' own `Included` flag is no longer offered. No migration.
- Open (**B**): while the report cannot be projected, the settings still show the five
  former switches, with no blocks.
- Open (**C**): read mode shows the settings as static ticks. This replaces R616's summary
  line.
- Open (**D**): the read-only blocks are pinned last, with the guide sentence in its print
  place, and have no move controls.
- Open (**E**): the commentary block's text box writes `report.valuation_commentary_text`.
- Open (**F**): the operator supplies the readiness and render blocker words that now say
  "under On the report on the Valuation section".
- Open (**G**): the settings are laid out in one wrapping line (proposed) or in columns.
- Open (**H**): the commentary tick reads "PAV commentary", its block's heading, rather than
  "Valuation commentary".
- Open (**I**): with the statement off, its block keeps the paragraphs that still print
  (R609).
