## 0. The brief and the scope

The operator requested a Stage 1 design and usability review across **every single page** on Pegasus Web (`src/Pegasus.Web` on `origin/dev`), scoped strictly to **mockups only**:

> *"Examine every single page on the app. See if there are any ways you can improve the design, UI, or UX"*
> *"Mockups only."*

Every routed page, shared layout, dialog, partial, stylesheet, script, and label owner under `src/Pegasus.Web` was examined line by line from two angles:

1. **Usability walk ("imagine you're a person trying to use it")**: Where does a click or `Enter` key do something surprising? Where does a save or filter reload lose scroll, selection, or lease state? Where is the operator asked to type a 36-character GUID instead of picking a person or Case? Where is the same fact printed twice or three times on the same screen? Where does a button open a dialog for the wrong row?
2. **Design authority & contract conformance**: Where do local page CSS or templates drift from `docs/design/README.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, and `.agents/skills/pegasus-ui-guardrails/SKILL.md` (`36px` control height, `40px` table row height, `11px` uppercase column headers, `20px` page title, no explanatory copy or ledes, one fact one home, no raw internal identifiers or parser names, no banned `"intake"` copy in UI)?
